SouMed · Student admissions

A look at the student admission workflow. Fields can vary with each school's configuration.

01 · Student journey

A complete picture of every student.

Keep admissions, student profiles, enrollment details, and family links together. Find a record, review the student's information, and manage it according to your role.

  • Record new admissions and essential student details.
  • Find student profiles and review enrollment status.
  • Import multiple student records where enabled.
Admissions · Student profiles · Enrollment
SouMed · Student attendance

Attendance screen from the training demo; the displayed learner record is synthetic.

02 · Learning & attendance

Keep teaching and class records moving.

Support day-to-day class administration with the tools to set up academic groups, take attendance, enter assessment marks, and review grade information.

  • Manage classes, sections, subjects, and timetables.
  • Record student attendance by class and date.
  • Enter marks and review grading progress.
Classes · Attendance · Grades
SouMed · Financial overview

Finance overview from the training demo. Amounts shown are sample values, not school results.

03 · Fees & finance

Bring collections and finance into view.

Manage student invoices and fee payments alongside school financial activity, with tools to review revenue, expenses, account balances, and finance reports.

  • Review student invoices, payments, and outstanding balances.
  • Record and review revenue, expenses, and account activity.
  • Track budgets and requisitions where configured.
Fees · Accounting · Budgets · Reports
SouMed · Employee records

Staff records screen from the training demo; access depends on role.

04 · People & operations

Keep the people and practicalities on track.

Support the staff processes and operational workflows that help a school run, from employee records and leave to payroll, library, events, and transport.

  • Maintain employee information and process leave workflows.
  • Review payroll and payslip information where available.
  • Support library and transport workflows when enabled.
HR · Payroll · Library · Transport
SouMed · Bulk SMS and email

Communication campaign filters from the training demo.

05 · Communication

Give school updates a connected home.

Use the communication tools available at your school to reach the intended groups and review campaign information. Parents and students can access the information provided for their accounts.

  • Send school messages or bulk SMS and email where configured.
  • Review campaign reports with authorized access.
  • Provide parent and student portal access by role.
Messages · SMS & email · Family portals
SouMed · Reports centre

Reports centre from the training demo. Available reports vary by school and role.

06 · Reports & insight

See the information behind the work.

Find relevant reports in one place and review records across the school. Each report provides its own filters and available print or export options.

  • Browse academic, attendance, and student reports.
  • Review finance, HR, and operational information.
  • Filter reports to the branch and academic context you need.
Academic · Attendance · Finance · Operations
