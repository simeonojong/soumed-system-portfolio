Finance requests & approvals

Submit, review, approve, and track finance requisitions through configured approval routes.

Where the finance requisition workflow is enabled, authorized staff can submit a request, review its progress, and follow the configured approval and fulfillment process.

What you can do

Features at a glance

  • Submit a requisition with the required department and supporting details.
  • Review, return, approve, or reject a request according to the assigned role.
  • Track request status and history.
  • Record fulfillment or payments where the workflow permits.
i

Availability depends on your school

Approval routes, request categories, and payment steps are configured for each school and may not be enabled everywhere.
