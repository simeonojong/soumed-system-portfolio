Module overview

Parent & student portals

Parents can select a linked child to review available attendance, grade sheets, invoices, events, and messages. Students can access their academic information and available tools such as homework, online exams, live classes, library, and messages.

Check availability at your school

What you can do

Features at a glance

  • Let parents switch between linked children.
  • Provide parent access to relevant attendance, grades, invoices, events, and messages.
  • Provide students access to class information, timetable, attendance, homework, and grades.
  • Enable additional student tools where the school's setup allows.
